Your games. Your orbit.

Rate the games you’ve played, watch your library become a solar system, and fly out to find what to play next.

In beta for iPhone and iPad. Join the beta on TestFlight

Rate what you’ve played.

Give each game one to five stars for Fun, Gameplay, Art and sound, and Story. Fun sets the range and the other three place the game within it, so a game you couldn’t put down never scores like one you tolerated.

Your list orders itself as you go. Keep notes, dates and platforms alongside each game.

Watch it become your orbit.

Every game you rank becomes a world orbiting your star. Higher-ranked games are larger worlds, and games you started earlier orbit closer.

Your star takes its color from the gaming eras you love most. Each world takes its look from its game.

Fly out for the next one.

Launch a discovery flight and your pilot visits up to five games picked for your taste, each with a briefing, trailers and your predicted score. Save the ones you want to play and pass on the rest.

You’ll see which friends have been there before you. Two flights a day are free.

Better with friends.

Add friends by handle or friend code. You’ll see each other’s scores and any notes you choose to share, and their ratings sharpen your recommendations. Invite friends to earn the Hamster and Penguin pilots.

Private where it matters.

Notes, tags and play dates stay yours unless you share a note, and you decide who sees your profile. No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.
