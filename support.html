GameOrbit support

How does ranking work?

Choose “I liked it,” “It was fine,” or “I didn’t like it,” then compare games within that group. Your score follows your ranking. “Too tough” creates a tie, “Skip” moves on, and Undo takes back your last choice. You can also save a game unranked.

What does my orbit show?

Each ranked game becomes a world. Higher-ranked games have larger planets; ties have the same size. Earlier start dates sit closer to your star. If no start date is recorded, the orbit uses the date the game was added. Distance shows order, not elapsed time. Select a world for its details, or switch to List for exact rankings.

Where are my games saved?

Guest libraries stay on your device. When you sign in, your account library syncs privately across devices. Adding existing guest games to an account is a separate choice. Sync does not publish your journal. Use Settings → Library to manage sync and review conflicts.

Who can see my profile and journal?

Profiles start Public and include your name, handle, bio, pilot, platforms, and a summary of your gaming taste. In Edit profile, choose Public, Friends, or Only me. Friends means people you follow who follow you back. Your journal stays private unless you publish an entry.

Published entries are separate copies. Later journal edits and profile visibility changes do not remove them. Use Account → Published games to update or remove a shared copy.

Can I export or restore my library?

Basic and Pro include library export. Backup import, recovery copies before replacing data, account deletion, and applicable personal-data requests remain free. Contact support to request your data. Restoring a backup replaces the current library; a signed-in library then syncs to other devices. Keep exports safe: they can contain private notes and saved reports.

How do memberships work?

When available, Basic and Pro are optional annual subscriptions through Apple. See the in-app offer for the current price and included features. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple subscription settings. Restore purchases uses the Apple Account that bought the membership; it does not restore your game library. Deleting GameOrbit or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings → Account and choose “Delete account and data.” This removes that account’s online data and its active library on this device. Other accounts and guest libraries are unchanged. Offline devices, system backups, and exported copies may still hold data. See the privacy policy for deletion details.

How do I report a player or post?

Use its menu to report or block. Reports are visible to the moderation team and the reporter. For an appeal, contact support. Read the community guidelines.

Can’t sign in?

Use a sign-in option shown in the app. For email codes, check your address and spam folder, wait before requesting another code, and use only the latest one. Never send support your password, sign-in code, or session token.
