GameOrbit support

How does ranking work?

Rate Fun, Gameplay, Art and sound, and Story from one to five stars. These ratings determine your score, with Fun counting a little more. Games with equal scores can be ordered or tied. Your scores stay the same as your library grows.

What does my orbit show?

Each ranked game becomes a world. Higher-ranked games have larger planets; ties have the same size. Earlier start dates sit closer to your star. If no start date is recorded, the orbit uses the date the game was added. Distance shows order, not elapsed time. Select a world for its details. Your Profile shows the ranked list beneath your star spectrum; era filters select games by release date.

Where are my games saved?

Sign in with Apple or Google before setup. Your account library saves automatically across devices and remains available offline after it has loaded. Settings shows any library changes that need your review.

Who can see my profile and journal?

Profiles start Public and include your name, handle, bio, pilot, platforms, and a summary of your gaming taste. In Edit profile, choose Public, Friends, or Only me. Friends means accepted friend requests. Played games, ranks, and scores follow your profile audience. Notes, tags, and play dates stay private unless you explicitly share a note.

Shared notes follow your profile audience. Later private edits do not change shared text until you share again. Use the note’s visibility control to make it private.

Can I get a copy of my data or recover my library?

Contact support for a copy of your data or help recovering a library. Applicable personal-data requests remain free. Use the same sign-in provider to access your existing account on a new device.

How do friends and invite credit work?

In Friends, tap Add friends to find a handle or code, or share your personal link. A friend request needs acceptance. Eligible new accounts can separately confirm an invite code for credit. Joining and saving a first game qualifies; a rating is not required. Your invites shows progress toward Hamster at two successful invites and Penguin at five.

How do memberships work?

When available, Basic and Pro are optional annual subscriptions through Apple. See the in-app offer for the current price and included features. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple subscription settings. Restore purchases uses the Apple Account that bought the membership; it does not restore your game library. Deleting GameOrbit or your account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings and choose “Delete account.” This removes that account’s online data and its active library on this device. Other accounts and guest libraries are unchanged. Offline devices, system backups, and exported copies may still hold data. See the privacy policy for deletion details.

How do I report a player or post?

Use its menu to report or block. Reports are visible to the moderation team and the reporter. For an appeal, contact support. Read the community guidelines.

Can’t sign in?

Use the same Apple or Google account you originally used. Never send support your password, sign-in code, or session token.
