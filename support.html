GameOrbit support

How does ranking work?

Choose “I liked it,” “It was fine,” or “I didn’t like it,” then compare games within that group. “Too tough” creates a tie; “Skip” moves on without deciding. Undo takes back your last comparison. You can save unranked and return later.

Where are my games saved?

Your library is saved on your device. Export a backup in Settings, or connect an account and explicitly upload a private cloud backup. Restoring replaces the current local library, so export a copy first if you want to keep both.

How do I remove a shared entry?

Open Account → Published games and remove its public copy. Editing a private journal entry does not automatically update or remove an earlier published copy.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile → Settings → Account, then choose “Delete account and data.” Export anything you want to keep first. Deletion removes your online account data and this device’s local library. Other devices and exported copies must be erased separately.

How do I report a player or post?

Use the menu on their profile or shared entry to report or block. Include enough context for review. Reports are private to the moderation team and reporter. Read our community guidelines.

Can’t sign in?

Check the email address and your spam folder, wait briefly before requesting another code, and use only the latest code. Your private local library remains available without signing in. Never send a password, one-time code, session token, or full library backup to support.
