Community guidelines

Talk about games with curiosity and respect. Make room for different tastes.

Keep people safe

No harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, or sharing someone’s private information. Do not impersonate others, spam, or post content you do not have the right to share.

Respect spoilers

Mark spoilers and keep details behind the spoiler warning. Critique games and ideas without targeting people.

Choose what you share

Your notes, tags, play dates, and collections stay private. You can explicitly share a note; later private edits do not update that shared text. Use the note’s visibility control to make it private.

Your profile, current played games, ranks, scores, and shared notes follow one audience setting. Profiles start Public; choose Friends or Only me in Edit profile. Friends means accepted friend requests.

Report, block, and appeal

Report concerning content from its menu. Blocking hides your profiles and entries from one another and removes friendships, pending requests, and likes between you. Reports are private to the moderation team and reporter. We may remove content or suspend accounts. Do not use reports to target someone for a different opinion.

Some harmful public text is screened automatically. Reports and human review still matter. Contact support to provide context or appeal a decision.
