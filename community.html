Make room for different tastes.

Good company.

Talk about games with curiosity and respect. A different favorite is an invitation to a conversation.

Keep people safe

Harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, and sharing someone’s private information are not allowed. Do not impersonate others, spam, or publish content you do not have the right to share.

Let people discover for themselves

Mark spoilers and keep details behind the spoiler warning. Critique games and ideas without targeting people.

You control your journal

Private notes stay on your device unless you upload a private backup or choose to publish a copy. Review the sharing preview before publishing. You can remove published copies from your account.

Report, block, and appeal

Report concerning content from its menu. Blocking hides your profiles and entries from one another and removes follows and likes between you. Reports are reviewed by the moderation operator, who can remove content or suspend accounts. Do not use reports to target someone for a different opinion. Contact support to provide additional context or appeal a decision.

Some harmful text is screened before publication. Automated screening does not catch everything; reports and human review remain necessary.
