Community guidelines

GameOrbit is for talking about games. Disagree about games as much as you like; be decent to the people.

Be respectful

No harassment, hate, threats or sexual content involving minors. Criticize games, not the people who like them.

Be yourself

Don’t impersonate anyone, spam, or post what you don’t have the right to share. Don’t post someone else’s private information.

Mark spoilers

If a shared note gives away a plot twist or ending, turn on its spoiler warning.

Share on purpose

Notes stay private until you share one. Your profile, ranked games and shared notes are visible to your profile audience: everyone, friends, or only you.

Reporting and blocking

Report anything that breaks these guidelines from its menu. Reports go to our moderators, not to the person reported. Blocking hides your profiles and posts from each other and removes any friendship, pending request and likes between you. Please don’t use reports to punish someone for an opinion.

Names, handles and shared notes are also checked automatically for harmful words. We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these guidelines. To explain a situation or appeal a decision, contact us.
