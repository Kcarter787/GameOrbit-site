Privacy policy

Last updated September 17, 2026. This policy describes the Gameprefs app and this website, operated by Gameprefs. You can contact the operator through the support page.

Your private library

The app stores your local profile, game entries, rankings, collections, platform preferences, tags, and notes on your device. Notes are private by default. You may export a copy, restore a backup, or erase local data in Settings. Exports contain private notes; share them carefully. Your system device backups may also include this library.

When you connect an account

We process your email address to sign you in with a one-time code, an account identifier, and the public handle, display name, and bio you choose. Session credentials are stored in your device’s Keychain. Your account profile is visible to other signed-in players, subject to blocking and moderation.

The service stores follows, likes, blocks, and reports to provide social features and prevent abuse. Reports are available to the moderation team and to the person who submitted them; they are not shown to the reported player.

What you publish

Publishing creates a separate public copy of the game entry, including the status, rank, preference group, platform, dates, tags, notes, and spoiler flag shown in the preview. Signed-in players can see that copy, subject to blocking and moderation. Later local edits stay private until you publish again. Remove shared copies under Account → Published games.

Optional cloud backup

Uploading a cloud backup stores your entire library, including private notes, in your account. Database access policies restrict that backup to your account and authorized service administration. It is encrypted in transit and by the hosting provider at rest; it is not end-to-end encrypted. Restoring a backup replaces the current library on that device.

Service providers and technical data

Supabase hosts authentication, the database, and server functions. Online catalog queries are sent through our server to IGDB/Twitch. Requests for game cover images go to the catalog’s image service. These services receive technical information needed to deliver the request, such as IP address and request metadata. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security.

We use the information to operate the app, personalize recommendations from your preferences, maintain security, resolve support requests, and moderate public content. We do not sell personal information. The app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you erase it or remove the app, subject to system backups. Account information remains while your account exists or until you remove the relevant shared copy. Account → Delete account and data removes the account, profile, published entries, relationships, private cloud backup, and local library on the device where you delete it. Signing out alone keeps your local library. Copies exported to other places and other devices’ offline libraries are not remotely erased.

Reports submitted by other players may remain after a reported account is deleted, with the account reference removed, for abuse review. Service logs and provider backups follow the providers’ retention settings and may remain temporarily after active data is deleted. We minimize support and moderation records to what is needed to resolve requests and protect the service.

Your choices and rights

You can use the private journal without an account, choose what to publish, block players, export your library, and delete your account in the app. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy requests that you cannot complete in the app, contact support. We may need to verify ownership before changing account data. Rights may vary by where you live.

Children and changes

The online service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their account information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information. We will update this page when our practices change and give appropriate notice of material changes.
