Privacy policy

Updated September 24, 2026. GameOrbit and this website are operated by Kevin Carter. For privacy questions or requests, email kevin.carter787@gmail.com.

Your library and account

GameOrbit stores your profile, games, rankings, dates, platforms, tags, notes, collections, preferences, and saved insights on your device. Sign-in with Apple or Google is required before setup. Device backups may also contain this information.

Signing in saves your account library privately and syncs it across devices. Saved insights can include report commentary you write; analytics do not automatically include journal notes. Current played games, ranks, and scores follow your profile audience; notes remain private unless explicitly shared. Conflicting changes may need your review, and recovery copies can preserve earlier data.

We process your account identifier, email address (including an Apple private relay address), and basic identity information from the sign-in option you use. Available sign-in providers authenticate under their own policies. We request identity information, not your address book or messages. Session credentials are kept in the device Keychain. The app does not retain Google API tokens.

Your profile and shared entries

Your profile contains your name, handle, bio, pilot, platforms, and a star spectrum with your played games, their release years, ranks, and scores. Profiles start Public and can be accessed without signing in. In Edit profile, choose Friends (people whose friend requests you have accepted, or who have accepted yours) or Only me. Blocks and moderation also restrict access. The spectrum and game list reflect your saved library. Your chosen audience can see the ranks and scores in that list. Journal notes, personal tags, and play dates are not included.

Sharing a note creates a copy of that note and its spoiler setting for your profile audience. Later private note edits do not update the shared text until you explicitly share again. Making a note private removes that copy. Profile visibility and blocks also govern shared notes and activity. Personal tags and play dates stay private. Copies someone has already saved cannot be recalled.

We store friend requests, friendships, likes, blocks, and reports to provide social features and prevent abuse. Reports are visible to the moderation team and reporter, not the reported player.

Recommendations, insights, and discovery

Your rankings and game preferences support recommendations and personal insights. Current recommendation calculations run on your device. Private notes are excluded from server recommendation inputs. Predicted scores are estimates, not ratings you have given. Friend averages use current scores from accepted friends whose profile audience admits you. Community averages use current scores from Public profiles.

For signed-in discovery flights, we store your account ID, a random flight ID, and the reveal time to enforce allowances and avoid counting retries twice. Older flight records are removed when you next check or use discovery after they fall outside the recent retention window of seven days before the current UTC day. They are also removed when the account is deleted.

Optional setup answers

You can skip platform selection and the first game during setup. Gaming familiarity, weekly playtime, and platforms help personalize the app. Previously collected discovery-channel answers help us understand how people find GameOrbit and do not affect recommendations. Unfinished setup progress is kept on your device; completed answers save to your account library.

You can edit platforms in Edit profile. Previously collected familiarity and playtime answers remain in your private account library. Familiarity, playtime, and discovery-channel answers are not added to your public profile. Profile platforms follow your chosen audience. No third-party onboarding analytics SDK is included.

Optional Steam connection

When importing is available and you choose to connect, Steam verifies your account on its own sign-in page. We receive your Steam account ID and read the games and playtime Steam makes visible. We never receive your Steam password or override its privacy settings. Unconfirmed import data expires after 24 hours and is removed by scheduled cleanup.

Only games you confirm enter your journal, without inferred ratings, notes, completion dates, or played platforms. Their Steam identifiers and observed playtime are saved privately, separately from rankings; playtime does not currently change recommendation weighting. Disconnecting removes the link, pending imports, and retained Steam activity, while confirmed journal entries remain. Deleting the synced library or account also removes that Steam activity. Minimal import receipts support safe retries.

Memberships and invitations

Apple handles membership payments; we do not receive payment-card details. The app checks purchase and renewal status to provide membership features. For paid server features, Apple’s signed membership confirmation is sent to our service and checked with Apple. These confirmations are processed transiently, not stored in your library or backups. Membership follows the Apple Account used to purchase it. Deleting your GameOrbit account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Confirming an invite code records the inviter separately from friendship. A friend request requires acceptance before either person gains Friends-only access. For invite rewards, we record whether a qualifying first game was saved, without showing the game or its notes to the inviter. The inviter can see your name, handle, and whether the invite is pending, successful, or closed. Authorized administration can review attribution and abuse. Invite links contain an identifier, not a sign-in credential. We do not upload contacts or send invitations automatically.

Deleting an account removes identifying invite activity and its account-bound rewards and links. Anonymous credit may remain so another player keeps a legitimately earned reward.

Providers and technical information

Supabase hosts authentication, the database, and server functions. Our primary database is in Oregon, United States. Apple and Google provide sign-in. Game searches and metadata requests pass through our server to IGDB/Twitch; Steam supplies some game media. Images and trailers load from their media hosts. GitHub Pages hosts this website.

These providers receive technical information needed to deliver and secure requests, such as IP addresses and request metadata. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. Processing and operational logs may occur in other locations under provider policies.

Catalog searches use shared result caches indexed by a hashed query, without an account identifier. Cached results expire after 15 minutes; expired records are removed during later cache writes. A random identifier created for each app service session and request counts enforce catalog limits. These limits use minute and daily windows, with expired records removed on later requests. They are separate from your account and journal.

We use information to operate and personalize GameOrbit, secure accounts, answer support requests, and moderate shared content. We do not sell personal information. GameOrbit contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Account data is encrypted in transit and at rest by the hosting provider; it is not end-to-end encrypted. Private library access is restricted to your account and authorized service administration. Local libraries use device file protection.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you erase it or remove the app, subject to system backups. Account data remains while the account exists unless you remove it. Recovery copies held for support are separate from the active synced library. Restoring an earlier library changes the account data that other devices receive.

Delete account removes the account, profile, published entries, relationships, private library, sync receipts, earlier cloud backups, and that account’s active workspace on the device used for deletion. Other accounts and guest libraries are unchanged. Apple-linked accounts require confirmation with Apple so we can revoke authorization; the revocation credentials are processed transiently.

Signing out removes a fully synced active account workspace. Unsynced changes remain protected and hidden for that account until you sign in again. Older migration and recovery files can remain in protected app storage; removing the app clears that storage. Offline devices cannot be remotely erased while offline. System backups and exports remain under your control.

Reports made by other players may remain with the deleted account reference removed. Support and moderation records are kept as needed to resolve requests and protect the service. Provider logs and backups may remain temporarily under the providers’ retention settings.

Your choices

You can choose your profile audience, share or unshare individual notes, manage friendships, block players, and delete your account in Settings. Contact support to request a copy of your data or help with recovery. Applicable personal-data requests remain free.

Contact support for access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your data. We may verify account ownership. Your rights may vary by location.

Children and updates

The online service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their account information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information. We will update this policy when our practices change and give appropriate notice of material changes.
