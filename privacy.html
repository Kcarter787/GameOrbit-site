Privacy policy

Last updated September 18, 2026. This policy describes the Gameprefs app and this website, operated by Gameprefs. You can contact the operator through the support page.

Your private library

The app stores your local profile, game entries, rankings, collections, platform preferences, tags, and notes on your device. Notes are private by default. You may export a copy, restore a backup, or erase local data in Settings. Exports contain private notes; share them carefully. Your system device backups may also include this library.

When you connect an account

You can sign in with Apple, Google, Discord, or an email code. We process an account identifier, the email address shared by your chosen sign-in provider (which may be an Apple private relay address), and the public handle, display name, and bio you choose. The provider may also send basic profile metadata to Supabase during sign-in; it is not automatically published as your Gameprefs profile. We request identity information only, not contacts, messages, or Discord played-game history. Discord does not currently make played-game history available to apps. Session credentials are stored in your device’s Keychain. Your account profile is visible to other signed-in players, subject to blocking and moderation.

The service stores follows, likes, blocks, and reports to provide social features and prevent abuse. Reports are available to the moderation team and to the person who submitted them; they are not shown to the reported player.

What you publish

Publishing creates a separate public copy of the game entry, including the status, rank, personal score, preference group, platform, dates, tags, notes, and spoiler flag shown in the preview. Signed-in players can see that copy, subject to blocking and moderation. Later local edits stay private until you publish again. Remove shared copies under Account → Published games.

Private account synchronization

When you sign in after the app explains private synchronization, games, journals, rankings, ties, collections and preferences are saved to your account and synchronized across devices. Existing guest games and libraries created before sync was available require an explicit attachment or setup choice. Guest use remains local. Private synchronization does not publish content or update existing public posts. Database access policies restrict private data to your account and authorized service administration. Data is encrypted in transit and by the hosting provider at rest; it is not end-to-end encrypted.

Local SwiftData storage keeps account libraries separate, including offline changes. Changes to different games can merge automatically. Conflicting versions require review; temporary recovery copies can be exported from Library settings. Ranking, status, preference, platform, tag and date signals are stored in structured form. The server exposes an account-authorized recommendation input that excludes private notes. Current recommendations are calculated on your device; a future server recommendation system is not yet operating.

Optional Steam connection

When Steam importing is available and you choose to connect, Steam verifies your Steam account identifier in its own sign-in page. Gameprefs does not receive your Steam password. At your request, our server reads games and playtime that Steam makes visible. Connection does not override Steam privacy settings. Unconfirmed import data expires after 24 hours and is removed by scheduled cleanup. Only games you select are added to your private journal, without a rating, preference, completion date, notes, or inferred played platform.

For games you choose to import, we retain Steam game identifiers and observed playtime privately with your account, separately from your rankings. This activity does not currently change recommendation weighting. Our primary database is hosted by Supabase in the United States (Oregon); server processing and provider operational logs may occur in other locations according to their policies. Disconnecting Steam cancels pending imports and removes the link and retained Steam activity; player-confirmed journal entries remain. Deleting your private synchronized library removes pending imports and Steam activity. Deleting your account also removes its Steam connection. Minimal import receipts support safe retry and contain no Steam password or API key.

Optional onboarding answers

You can skip every setup question. Gaming familiarity, recent weekly playtime, and available platforms provide private context for getting started; your rankings tell us what you enjoy. The optional question about where you heard of Gameprefs helps us understand discovery channels and does not affect your recommendations. Guest answers stay on your device. When you sign in and enable private library synchronization, those answers can be attached to your account. Existing account answers take precedence. You can change familiarity, weekly playtime, and platforms in Edit profile. These answers are not added to your public community profile, and acquisition details are excluded from recommendation inputs. Setup progress stays on the device. No third-party onboarding analytics SDK is included.

Earlier cloud backups and exports

Earlier optional cloud backups contain the entire library, including private notes. Restoring one replaces the current account library, which then synchronizes to other devices. These backups are separate from the active synchronized library and are deleted when the account is deleted. Portable exports are under your control and can contain private notes.

Service providers and technical data

Supabase hosts authentication, the database, and server functions. If you select Apple, Google, or Discord sign-in, that provider authenticates you under its own privacy policy. Provider client secrets stay on the server; Gameprefs session credentials use the device Keychain. The app does not retain Google or Discord API tokens. Online catalog queries are sent through our server to IGDB/Twitch. Requests for game cover images go to the catalog’s image service. These services receive technical information needed to deliver the request, such as IP address and request metadata. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security.

We use the information to operate the app, personalize recommendations from your preferences, maintain security, resolve support requests, and moderate public content. We do not sell personal information. The app contains no advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Local information remains until you erase it or remove the app, subject to system backups. Account information remains while your account exists or until you remove the relevant shared copy. Account → Delete account and data removes the account, profile, published entries, relationships, structured private library, synchronization receipts, earlier cloud backups, and that account’s active saved workspace on the device where you delete it. Other accounts and guest games are unchanged. Library settings also lets you delete the active private synchronized library while keeping the account; this leaves published posts and earlier cloud backups intact. It retains a minimal account control record to prevent delayed uploads from recreating deleted content. For an Apple-linked account, we ask you to confirm with Apple and revoke Gameprefs’ Apple authorization before deleting the account. This confirmation code and the token used for revocation are processed transiently on the server and are not stored in the game library. Signing out removes a fully synchronized active account workspace. Unsynced changes stay hidden for that account until you sign in again; you can remove that saved copy in Library settings. Older migration and corruption-recovery files may remain in protected local application storage; removing the app clears that storage. System backups and copies exported elsewhere remain under your control. Other devices’ offline copies cannot be remotely erased while offline. On reconnecting after a synchronized-library deletion, the app blocks stale uploads and requires a deliberate choice before any preserved games can be added again.

Reports submitted by other players may remain after a reported account is deleted, with the account reference removed, for abuse review. Service logs and provider backups follow the providers’ retention settings and may remain temporarily after active data is deleted. We minimize support and moderation records to what is needed to resolve requests and protect the service.

Your choices and rights

You can use the private journal without an account, choose what to publish, block players, export your library, and delete your account in the app. For access, correction, deletion, or privacy requests that you cannot complete in the app, contact support. We may need to verify ownership before changing account data. Rights may vary by where you live.

Children and changes

The online service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their account information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information. We will update this page when our practices change and give appropriate notice of material changes.
